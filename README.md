# Proactive

Proactive is a to-do list and bookmark manager in one to take control my on-line life.

## Introduction

Every once in a while, I should visit/login to many on-line communities, SaaS, self-hosted web applications, or just random web-sites I found online. If I don't, My session will expire, I may forgot what I have, I may miss out useful information, and I may not performing needed actions in time. However, if I check a site too often, it is a waste of my time and will bore me to hell. Different sites need different checkining intervals, some should be checked monthly, like my on-line banks, some should be check weekly, like forums, and some should be checked annually, for stuffes that moved at a very slow pace. 

I cannot rely on notifications or emails sent from those sites, because many sites have a dark agenda that try to maximize my engagement to the detriment of my time managment and mental health. On the other hand, many ethical sites do not send notifications or don't even have a way to send notifications. Furthermore, spending significant amount of time responding to notifications and email reminders is reactive and makes browsering a chore instead of an exploration as it supposed to be. I want to be proactive and take control my on-line life; so polling all the sites with different intervals is the only viable approach I can see: Thus, I wrote Proactive.

Proactive is supposed to be used as a pinned page or browser homepage. The main branch will be hosted ... It is a javascript PWA, with no backend, no login, and all user data stored in the browser. Supposedly, one use one computer 99% of the time, your data will naver leave your browser. In case one want to move to another device or backup my data, the save and restore functionalies can be utilized. All are welcome to use my instance. Since the software is free, one can also clone it and host it somewhere else; it is all upto you. 

## Disclaimer

The software is tailored to my needs and may never become a full-featured to-do list or bookmark manager. I use it everyday, your mileage may vary.
